About HOTELI POA

Hoteli Poa is a modern Kenyan restaurant and food delivery platform built to serve authentic local cuisine with the speed and reliability our customers deserve. From the sizzling nyama choma of Nairobi to the coastal flavours of Mombasa, we bring the best of Kenya's kitchen to your table — whether you dine in, take away, or have it delivered.

How We Operate

Fresh, daily. Every dish starts with ingredients sourced each morning from trusted local markets and suppliers. Nothing sits, nothing waits.

Cooked to order. We don't pre-cook. When you place an order, our kitchen fires it up from scratch. That's why it tastes like home.

Fast and honest. Our kitchen gives you a real estimate, not a marketing number. If we're busy, we tell you. If we're on time, we deliver on time.

Every customer counts. Whether you're a regular or ordering for the first time, you get the same care, the same portions, and the same welcome.

What We Stand For

  • Quality first. No shortcuts in the kitchen.
  • Local roots. We support Kenyan farmers, suppliers, and riders.
  • Fair prices. Good food shouldn't be a luxury.
  • Real service. A real person answers your message, every time.

Our Promise

If your order is late, we tell you why. If something isn't right, we fix it. If you're happy, we want to hear about it.
